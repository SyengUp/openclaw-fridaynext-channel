## 英文模板

**Title / 开头第一行：**

```
📱 FridayNext — a native iOS client for your OpenClaw gateway

Your iPhone as a full OpenClaw channel + node. Install in one command:
curl -fsSL https://gw.syengup.host/v1/friday-next/install.sh | sh
```

**正文：**

```
I built a native iOS app that turns your iPhone into a full OpenClaw client —
streaming answers, transparent reasoning/tool calls, and your agent one tap away.

What makes it different:
- 📲 Native iOS, not a web wrapper (pure UIKit, App Store listed)
- 🎨 Interactive canvas — agents push live web content & dashboards into chat
- ⏰ Scheduled tasks from your phone
- 🔔 Proactive notifications when your agent needs you
- 📎 iOS share sheet — send links, text, photos, files to your agent anywhere
- 🔊 Built-in TTS to listen to answers
- 🗂️ Multi-agent & multi-server support
- 🌍 Optional FridayTunnel relay to reach your gateway away from home

Self-hosted & private by default — talks to your own gateway, no middleman.

Install:
curl -fsSL https://gw.syengup.host/v1/friday-next/install.sh | sh

Then open FridayNext and follow the in-app setup. One command, no CLI needed after that.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fridaynext/id6768689875 (free)
Source: https://github.com/SyengUp/openclaw-fridaynext-channel
ClawHub: https://clawhub.ai/syengup/plugins/friday-channel-next

Happy to answer questions or take feedback! 🦞
```
